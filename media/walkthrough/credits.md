# Copilot plan and credit budget

GitHub Copilot plans include a monthly allowance of GitHub AI Credits:

| Plan | AI credits / month |
|---|---|
| Copilot Pro | 1,000 |
| Copilot Pro+ | 3,900 |
| Copilot Max | 10,000 |
| Copilot Business | 1,900 per seat |
| Copilot Enterprise | 3,900 per seat |

Picking your plan pre-fills:

- the **monthly budget** shown with a pace projection on the Overview,
- the **renewal day** when the billing period starts,
- the **cost per credit** (plan price ÷ credits), used to turn credits into money.

All three stay editable. Check your plan on GitHub if the numbers changed.
